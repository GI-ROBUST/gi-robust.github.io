This privacy policy applies to the ROBUST 2026 workshop website at
`gi-robust.github.io/robust26`. It explains what personal data is processed
when you visit these pages, on what legal basis, and what rights you have.

This is a static website. **We set no cookies, run no analytics, embed no
tracking pixels, and operate no contact forms.** The processing described below
happens because your browser must fetch files from our hosting provider and one
content delivery network in order to display the page.

## Hosting — GitHub Pages

This website is hosted by:

GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA

When you access the site, GitHub's servers automatically record the following
in log files:

- your IP address
- the address of the previously visited page (referer request header)
- the date and time of the request
- the time zone difference to Greenwich Mean Time
- the content of the request
- the HTTP status code
- the amount of data transferred
- the website from which the request originates
- information about your browser and operating system

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure
and efficient provision of this website. We have no direct access to these log
files, and no tracking takes place. The data is deleted once the purpose of
processing ceases to apply.

**Transfer to a third country:** Data is processed in the USA. GitHub applies
the EU Standard Contractual Clauses to its worldwide processing activities
involving the personal data of EU residents. See GitHub's
[Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)
and [Data Protection Agreement](https://github.com/customer-terms/github-data-protection-agreement).

The Bavarian data protection supervisory authority takes the view that hosting
a purely static website for self-presentation purposes — with no transfer of
personal data and no tracking — does not require a separate data processing
agreement.

## Web fonts

This site uses the "Open Sans" typeface. **The font files are hosted on this
website itself and are delivered together with the rest of the page.** Your
browser makes no request to Google Fonts or to any other font provider, and no
data is transmitted to a third party in order to display the text.

## Content delivery network — jsDelivr

The Bootstrap stylesheet and JavaScript used for the page navigation are
delivered through the open-source CDN jsDelivr, operated by:

Prospect One, Krolewska 65A, 30-081 Kraków, Poland<br>
Privacy policy: [https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net)

jsDelivr serves these files through the edge networks of Fastly and Cloudflare.
A content delivery network is a set of geographically distributed servers that
optimises the delivery of content. In the course of this delivery, your IP
address and request metadata may be processed in the CDN's server logs.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the reliable
and fast delivery of these resources without operating a CDN of our own. This
processing is neither legally nor contractually required; without it, the
functionality of the website cannot be guaranteed.

You have the right to object to this processing. Whether an objection succeeds
depends on a balancing of the competing interests.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we process about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
- **erasure** of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
- **restriction** of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- **data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR)
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, contact the controller or the data protection
officer named above.
